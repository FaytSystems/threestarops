Restaurant inventory and kitchen management software

Chef Ledger by ThreeStarOps connects the records a restaurant creates every day: stock counts, prep sheets, vendor orders, recipes, and team schedules.

Count stock and manage par levels

Build your inventory around your own ingredients, vendors, units, stations, and storage locations. Keep stock counts and par levels together so the kitchen has a clearer starting point for restocking and ordering.

Plan prep and vendor orders

Use prep sheets, station records, vendor deadlines, and order sheets to organize the work before service. Higher plans add delivery history and POS CSV tools for planning from recorded restaurant sales.

Keep recipes and team records together

Maintain recipe and plate records, organize files and pictures where supported by your plan, and use staff scheduling tools to coordinate kitchen work. Plan limits and included features are shown before subscription checkout.
